2. Stakeholder Requirements
CEO
Business Goal
Monitor overall company performance.
Key Questions
•	Is the business growing?
•	Which regions generate the highest revenue?
•	Is profitability improving?
•	Which product categories drive growth?
KPIs
•	Total Sales
•	Total Profit
•	Profit Margin %
•	Year-over-Year Growth
•	Sales by Region
Reporting Frequency
Monthly Executive Review

CFO
Business Goal
Monitor financial performance.
Key Questions
•	Which categories generate the most profit?
•	Where are we losing money?
•	What is the impact of discounts?
•	Which regions have the lowest margins?
KPIs
•	Total Profit
•	Profit Margin
•	Discount %
•	Category Profit
•	Regional Profit
Reporting Frequency
Monthly Finance Report

COO
Business Goal
Improve operational performance.
Key Questions
•	Which regions perform below expectations?
•	Which shipping methods are used most?
•	Are delivery timelines consistent?
KPIs
•	Orders by Region
•	Shipping Mode Distribution
•	Average Delivery Time
•	Regional Performance
Reporting Frequency
Monthly Operations Report

Head of Sales
Business Goal
Increase revenue and improve sales performance.
Key Questions
•	Which products sell the most?
•	Which customers generate the highest revenue?
•	Which regions need attention?
•	Which categories are underperforming?
KPIs
•	Total Sales
•	Top Customers
•	Top Products
•	Regional Sales
•	Category Sales
Reporting Frequency
Weekly & Monthly Sales Reports

Regional Sales Managers
Business Goal
Improve regional sales.
Key Questions
•	Which cities perform best?
•	Which customers contribute the most revenue?
•	Which products are most popular?
KPIs
•	Regional Sales
•	City Sales
•	Customer Sales
•	Product Sales
Reporting Frequency
Weekly

Finance Team
Business Goal

Key Questions
•	Are profit calculations accurate?
•	Are discounts affecting profitability?
•	Which categories require cost review?
KPIs

•	Profit
•	Discount Analysis

Key Questions

•	Are KPI definitions consistent?

5. Stakeholder Success Criteria
Each stakeholder should be able to:
•	Access accurate and validated data.
•	Understand KPIs without ambiguity.
•	Make business decisions using standardized reports.
•	Receive reports at the agreed reporting frequency.
•	Trust that metrics are calculated consistently.
