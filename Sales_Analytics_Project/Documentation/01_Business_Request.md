Business Background
Apex Retail Solutions operates across multiple regions, serving both business and retail customers. While transactional sales data is available, reporting is fragmented and time-consuming, making it difficult for leadership to monitor business performance and make timely decisions.
The company requires a centralized SQL-based analytics solution to improve visibility into sales, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and regional trends.

Business Problem
Current reporting relies on manual spreadsheet preparation and inconsistent business logic.
As a result:
•	Sales performance is difficult to monitor consistently.
•	Profitability trends are not clearly visible.
•	High-performing and underperforming products are not identified efficiently.
•	Customer purchasing behavior is not analyzed regularly.
•	Regional performance comparisons are delayed.
•	Decision-makers lack a single source of truth.


Business Objective
Develop a production-ready SQL analytics solution that:
•	Provides reliable KPI reporting.
•	Enables sales performance monitoring.
•	Tracks profitability across products and regions.
•	Supports customer and product analytics.
•	Produces reusable SQL reports for business stakeholders.
•	Serves as the data foundation for Power BI dashboards.

Assumptions

•	Required fields are available for analysis.
•	Data quality issues will be identified during profiling and validation.
•	Stakeholders agree on KPI definitions before reporting.

Risks

•	Duplicate records.

•	Business rule violations.

Success Criteria
The project will be considered successful when:

•	SQL queries produce accurate and validated results.
•	Business insights are supported by data.
•	Reports are reusable.
•	Data is ready for Power BI integration.
•	Documentation is complete and suitable for analyst handover.
