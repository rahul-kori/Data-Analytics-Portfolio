Business Questions
Business questions are the foundation of every analysis. A Data Analyst should never start writing SQL queries without knowing which business questions need to be answered.

Each question below will later be mapped to SQL queries, KPIs, and Power BI visuals.

Business Questions
1. Executive (CEO)

Objective
Evaluate overall business performance and identify strategic opportunities.
Business Questions
•	What are the total sales and total profit?
•	Is the business growing over time?
•	Which regions contribute the highest revenue?
•	Which product categories generate the highest profit?
•	Which regions require immediate management attention?

2. Finance (CFO)

Objective
Monitor financial performance and profitability.
Business Questions
•	What is the overall profit margin?
•	Which categories generate the highest profit?
•	Which products are generating losses?
•	How do discounts affect profitability?
•	Which regions have the lowest profit margins?

3. Sales Department

Objective
Improve sales performance.
Business Questions
•	Which products generate the highest sales?
•	Which customers generate the highest revenue?
•	Which customer segments contribute the most sales?
•	Which regions have the strongest sales performance?
•	Which products should receive more sales focus?

4. Operations (COO)

Objective
Improve operational efficiency.
Business Questions
•	Which shipping modes are used most frequently?
•	What is the average shipping time?
•	Are there delivery delays?
•	Which regions experience operational bottlenecks?

5. Customer Analytics

Objective
Understand customer purchasing behavior.
Business Questions
•	Who are the top customers by sales?
•	Who are the most profitable customers?
•	Which customer segment contributes the highest revenue?
•	Which customers receive the highest discounts?
•	Which customers place the largest orders?

6. Product Analytics

Objective
Evaluate product performance.
Business Questions
•	Which products generate the highest sales?
•	Which products generate the highest profit?
•	Which products generate losses?
•	Which categories perform best?
•	Which sub-categories require improvement?

7. Regional Analytics

Objective
Compare business performance across locations.
Business Questions
•	Which region has the highest sales?
•	Which region has the highest profit?
•	Which states contribute the most revenue?
•	Which cities have the strongest performance?
•	Which locations require business improvement?

8. Time Series Analytics

Objective
Identify trends and seasonality.
Business Questions
•	How do monthly sales change over time?
•	How does monthly profit change over time?
•	Which months generate the highest sales?
•	Which quarters perform best?
•	Are there seasonal sales patterns?

9. Data Quality

Objective
Ensure reliable reporting.
Business Questions
•	Are there duplicate records?
•	Are there missing values?
•	Are data types correct?
•	Are there invalid dates?
•	Do all business rules pass validation?
