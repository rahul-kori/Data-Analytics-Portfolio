Success Metrics
Success Metrics define how the business will measure whether this analytics project is successful. They are different from KPIs:

KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) measure business performance (e.g., Sales, Profit, Profit Margin).
Success Metrics measure whether the analytics solution meets business and stakeholder expectations.

•	Data quality issues are documented and resolved where appropriate.
